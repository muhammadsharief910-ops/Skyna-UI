# Skyna image direction

Tool: built-in image generation (`image_gen`). Six images were generated for the original design, with eight additional assets for Sunroom. No images from the original Skyna website are loaded by the React app.

All images are photorealistic editorial travel scenes, with warm natural lighting, believable photographic detail, restrained cinematic color, and no text, logos, borders, watermarks, or collage. They are illustrative brand imagery.

## Kashmir hero — `public/images/kashmir-hero.webp`

Create a wide cinematic travel photograph to serve as the hero background for Skyna, an Indian travel website. Photorealistic editorial landscape, immersive widescreen 16:9 composition, high resolution. Serene Dal Lake inspired Kashmir landscape at sunrise, enormous snow-capped Himalayan peaks across upper half, pine-forested slopes with lovely autumn foliage, soft golden early light and atmospheric depth, tranquil dark teal water across lower half. A single small traditional Kashmiri shikara boat with warm saffron canopy on the right third, reflected in the water, elegant and peaceful. The left half has darker forest mountain slopes and uncluttered deep lake water, naturally suitable for white text overlay added later in HTML. No text, no typography, no logo, no border, no collage. Realistic camera photographic textures, sophisticated muted greens and golds, inviting premium boutique-travel mood, no oversaturation. Show just the photograph, not a website mockup.
